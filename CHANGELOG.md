# Changelog

## Unreleased

The first version of drift:

- Write and send messages: a composer in every chat and channel (Enter sends,
  Shift+Enter for a new line)

- Sign in with Microsoft (work or school account) in a small window; the sign-in
  is kept in the system keychain and renewed on its own
- Tabs in the top bar (Activity, Chats, Teams, Meetings) with unread counts, today's
  meeting with *Join*, and search
- One list of chats and channels, newest first, with previews and times; chips for
  unread, channels and people
- Chats as bubbles (yours on the right), grouped by author, with day pills; channel
  posts link to their thread, which opens beside the channel for replying
- Right-click a message to react, reply in its thread, copy it or open its link;
  click a reaction to add or take back yours
- Search messages in all chats and channels from the search field; a hit opens that
  message
- Links in messages open again; reactions hang off their bubble
- Find in the open conversation (⌘F); who's in it, with a click to your chat with them
- An emoji picker with Teams' colour emoji; emoji you send go as Teams emoji
- A cleaner search field: centred, outlined while typing, × or Esc to clear
- Activity: mentions and replies with who, what and where; a click opens that message
- Click a quoted message to go to it
- Reply with a quote, edit (↑ in an empty box edits your last message) and delete your
  messages; Markdown and @mentions when writing
- Teams' own colour emoji in reactions and messages
- Pin and unpin from the list's right-click menu; fold any section of the list, or all
  of them at once
- Cards from connectors (Azure Repos pull requests and the like) with their title as a
  link, their status and their facts (branches, reviewers)
- Opening a conversation marks it read in Teams too
- Messages keep their formatting: bold, italics, code, links, mentions and emoji;
  reactions under them; cards from bots and connectors (pull requests, alerts) as one
  compact row that opens its link
- Panels floating on a canvas, a dark and a warm light theme (or follow the system),
  violet accent, Geist
- ⌘R / Ctrl+R fetches everything again
